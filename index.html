What happened to the other controls

  • High Risk Process - Certificates / Permits→Treat as supporting controlImportant for authorisation and evidence, but not a strong standalone critical control because it does not itself prevent a fall unless linked to physical controls.
  • Workplace Inspections→Merge into CC01 and CC04Useful pre-work check. It should trigger field verification, not sit as an independent critical barrier.
  • EWP / Scissor lift secondary controls→Merge into CC03Secondary controls are a supporting requirement for MEWP selection, rescue and operation.
  • Emergency Response and Incident Management Team→Treat as supporting mitigating controlNeeded for consequence management but does not prevent the unwanted event. It should be tested through emergency management processes.
  • PPE, cyclone, lightning, cleaning and business continuity controls→Treat as supporting controlsRelevant to specific scenarios but not suitable as standalone critical controls for the primary fatal or serious harm pathways.

CC01

Working at Heights Equipment and Securing Objects

Retain as critical controlCurrent hierarchy: AdministrativeRecommended: Engineering supported by Administrative2 gaps identified

Objective: Provide a reliable, repeatable barrier for working at heights equipment and securing objects so the likelihood of Person / Object Falling from Height is reduced for Risk ID 9125. Control type: Preventing. Current hierarchy: Administrative. Recommended hierarchy: Engineering supported by Administrative controls. Pathway linkage: Causes 17826, 17828, 17830 and working near or over water exposure.

Performance standard gaps

  • The current hierarchy is administrative although the control includes physical equipment, fall restraint and tool security.
  • Current testing requires records and infield verification but lacks explicit pass, fail and escalation triggers.

Verification coverage

CCV: Control Owner or delegate; minimum six monthly unless monthly requirement is stated. It checks equipment management systems, competency, task evidence and escalation.

FCCV: Supervisor, Control Owner delegate or competent verifier; point of work. It checks equipment condition, worker understanding, secured tools and changed conditions.

Revised performance standard

  • Select suitable fall restraint, fall arrest, access equipment and dropped object controls before work starts (CC01-D-01).
  • Complete pre-use checks of fall protection equipment, tooling retention and access equipment (CC01-O-01).
  • Verify WAH equipment management systems at least six monthly (CC01-T-01).
  • Confirm WAH, rope access or near-water competency is current (CC01-C-01).
  • Retain risk assessment, JHA, permit or certificate, inspection and rescue information (CC01-E-01).
  • Pass when equipment is certified or inspected, correctly anchored, tooling is secured and workers can explain controls (CC01-P-01).
  • Fail when equipment is damaged, out of date, unsuitable, workers are not competent or dropped object controls are absent (CC01-F-01).
  • Stop the task, restrict access and escalate when WAH controls are missing or ineffective (CC01-ESC-01).
  • Reverify after scope, weather, rescue plan or equipment changes, or after any WAH incident (CC01-RV-01).

CC02

Building and Fixed Plant Design Standards

Retain as critical controlCurrent hierarchy: Engineering2 gaps identified

Objective: Provide a reliable, repeatable barrier for building and fixed plant design standards so the likelihood of Person / Object Falling from Height is reduced for Risk ID 9125. Control type: Preventing. Hierarchy: Engineering. Pathway linkage: Causes 17826, 17828, 17830 and static dropped object pathways.

Performance standard gaps

  • The current standard references 100-PR-ST-0001, but that source was not supplied, so monitoring and review requirements cannot be fully verified.
  • The current testing covers removal certificates, but design requirements do not explicitly require temporary controls before removal.

Verification coverage

CCV: Control Owner or delegate; minimum six monthly unless monthly requirement is stated. It checks structural records, defects, change management and anchor point evidence.

FCCV: Supervisor, Control Owner delegate or competent verifier; point of work. It checks access integrity, temporary controls, removal certificates and anchor point tags.

Revised performance standard

  • Design accessways, walkways, stairs, ladders, handrails, flooring, anchor points and guarding to remove open edge and static dropped object exposure so far as reasonably practicable (CC02-D-01).
  • Maintain complete, serviceable access systems free from uncontrolled open edges and degraded structural items (CC02-O-01).
  • Verify maintenance history, planned inspections, fatality risk defect status and change events at least six monthly (CC02-T-01).
  • Use personnel competent in structural integrity and site exposure controls (CC02-C-01).
  • Keep inspection, defect, work order, change approval and anchor point records (CC02-E-01).
  • Pass when access systems are complete, defects have controls and modifications have approved change events (CC02-P-01).
  • Fail when flooring, handrails, stairs, ladders, anchor points or structural elements create uncontrolled exposure (CC02-F-01).
  • Restrict access, barricade or remove from service and raise urgent work order for uncontrolled defects (CC02-ESC-01).
  • Reverify after floor, grid mesh, roof panel or handrail removal, modification, damage, corrosion finding or return to service (CC02-RV-01).

CC03

Mobile Equipment and Plant Design and Operating Standards

Retain as critical controlCurrent hierarchy: Engineering2 gaps identified

Objective: Provide a reliable, repeatable barrier for mobile equipment and plant design and operating standards so the likelihood of Person / Object Falling from Height is reduced for Risk ID 9125. Control type: Preventing. Hierarchy: Engineering. Pathway linkage: Causes 17827 and 17828 involving EWP or scissor lift operation and open edge exposure.

Performance standard gaps

  • The current performance standard refers to itinerant plant but does not fully state required owner documents.
  • The current standard lacks explicit field criteria for overreach, ground conditions and rescue.

Verification coverage

CCV: Control Owner or delegate; minimum six monthly unless monthly requirement is stated. It checks ACE approval, owner records, maintenance systems and tag out or removal response.

FCCV: Supervisor, Control Owner delegate or competent verifier; point of work. It checks ground and task envelope, approval sticker, operator competency, rescue readiness, prestart and logbook status.

Revised performance standard

  • Select EWPs, scissor lifts, work boxes and related plant for ground conditions, task height, load, outreach, rescue and site conditions (CC03-D-01).
  • Confirm ACE approval, current prestart, logbook and inspection evidence before site use (CC03-O-01).
  • Verify maintenance strategy, site approval status and infield MEWP control application at required intervals (CC03-T-01).
  • Confirm operators, spotters, inspectors and certificate holders are trained and competent (CC03-C-01).
  • Keep registration, inspection, maintenance, logbook and WLL or MRC marking evidence (CC03-E-01).
  • Pass when plant has current evidence, suitable condition, competent operator and controls for ground, overreach and rescue (CC03-P-01).
  • Fail when approval is missing, inspections are expired, plant is damaged, ground is unsuitable or controls are not understood (CC03-F-01).
  • Tag out, remove from service or remove from site when approval, inspection or condition requirements are not met (CC03-ESC-01).
  • Reverify when inspection expires, plant returns to site, task height or ground changes, damage occurs or after rescue use (CC03-RV-01).

CC04

Physical Separation (Barricading) and Platform Ladders

Retain as critical controlCurrent hierarchy: Engineering2 gaps identified

Objective: Provide a reliable, repeatable barrier for physical separation (barricading) and platform ladders so the likelihood of Person / Object Falling from Height is reduced for Risk ID 9125. Control type: Preventing. Hierarchy: Engineering. Pathway linkage: Cause 17828 and dropped object exposure pathway.

Performance standard gaps

  • The current control references the Physical Separation procedure, which was not supplied, so exclusion zone design and barricade selection details cannot be fully verified.
  • Annual platform ladder verification may not confirm point of work condition.

Verification coverage

CCV: Control Owner or delegate; minimum six monthly unless monthly requirement is stated. It checks dropped object review, exclusion zone installation, secured openings and SIMOPS communication.

FCCV: Supervisor, Control Owner delegate or competent verifier; point of work. It checks drop zone access, passive controls, secured materials and weather or SIMOPS suitability.

Revised performance standard

  • Design exclusion zones, barricades, signage, toe boards, covers, netting, platforms and platform ladders so a falling object cannot strike a worker (CC04-D-01).
  • Install barricades, signage, access restrictions, secured tools and materials, covered gratings and suitable platform ladders before work starts (CC04-O-01).
  • Verify dropped object review, controls, SIMOPS notification, exclusion zones, signage and weather suitability monthly (CC04-T-01).
  • Use a verifier who understands dropped object pathways, barricade selection and platform ladder condition requirements (CC04-C-01).
  • Keep drop review, barricade placement, warning signs, access restriction, tool security and platform ladder inspection evidence (CC04-E-01).
  • Pass when the exclusion zone prevents worker exposure and work groups know the restrictions (CC04-P-01).
  • Fail when a worker can enter the drop zone, tools are unsecured, openings are uncovered, ladders are defective or signage is missing (CC04-F-01).
  • Stop work, expand the exclusion zone and escalate if separation is ineffective (CC04-ESC-01).
  • Reverify when weather changes, SIMOPS are introduced, layout changes or work moves to another level (CC04-RV-01).

CC05

Scaffolding Integrity and Compliance

Retain as critical controlCurrent hierarchy: Engineering2 gaps identified

Objective: Provide a reliable, repeatable barrier for scaffolding integrity and compliance so the likelihood of Person / Object Falling from Height is reduced for Risk ID 9125. Control type: Preventing. Hierarchy: Engineering. Pathway linkage: Cause 17829 involving scaffold erection, modification, use, inspection and dismantling.

Performance standard gaps

  • The scaffold design criteria reference the scaffolding procedure, but design loading, support assessment and mixed system controls need to be explicit.
  • The testing approach includes quarterly sampling and a 30-day plan, but adverse weather and post-repair reinspection triggers need to be included.

Verification coverage

CCV: Control Owner or delegate; minimum six monthly unless monthly requirement is stated. It checks scaffold work packs, inspection tags, 30-day inspection triggers and scaffolder competency.

FCCV: Supervisor, Control Owner delegate or competent verifier; point of work. It checks scaffold tag status, match to work pack, physical edge protection and reverification after trigger events.

Revised performance standard

  • Define scaffold load rating, decking area, concentrated load, supported equipment, environmental loading and support locations before construction (CC05-D-01).
  • Erect, alter and dismantle scaffolds with competent scaffolders, tag them ready for use, mark load capacity and record them in a scaffold register (CC05-O-01).
  • Inspect scaffolds at least every 30 days and sample quarterly for design, tag, register and defect status (CC05-T-01).
  • Confirm scaffolders hold the required HRWL class and working at height competency for the scaffold type (CC05-C-01).
  • Keep scaffold work pack, design loads, risk assessment, mixed system approval where relevant, support assessment and inspection history (CC05-E-01).
  • Pass when the scaffold is complete, tagged, in date, within design load, inspected, registered and has compliant access and competent scaffolders (CC05-P-01).
  • Fail when scaffold is untagged, out of date, altered without approval, overloaded, damaged, incomplete or erected by non-competent persons (CC05-F-01).
  • Prevent access, apply Do Not Use status or equivalent and escalate unsafe scaffold conditions (CC05-ESC-01).
  • Reverify after adverse weather, repair, modification, incident, support change, mixed system change or stability concern (CC05-RV-01).
